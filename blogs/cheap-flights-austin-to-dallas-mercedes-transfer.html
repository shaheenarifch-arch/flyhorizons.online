Which Dallas airport: DAL or DFW?

  • Dallas Love Field (DAL) is about 6 miles from downtown. It's the easy pick for downtown, Uptown, the Medical District and Park Cities. It's also Southwest's home base.
  • DFW International is about 20 miles from downtown, between Dallas and Fort Worth. It suits Irving, Las Colinas, Grapevine and Fort Worth. It's American Airlines' main hub, so there are more flight times to choose from.

Fares between the two airports can differ a lot on the same day, so search both before you book.

How to book it, step by step

  1. Search Austin (AUS) to Dallas on Evago and compare DAL and DFW for your dates.
  2. Book a few weeks ahead if you can. Mid-week flights are often cheaper than Friday and Sunday flights.
  3. Note your landing time and airport, then book your LandJet Mercedes transfer for that time: an S-Class for 1–2 people or a Sprinter van for groups.
  4. Keep both confirmations on your phone. If your flight changes, update the transfer time straight away.

Tips for the Austin–Dallas route

  • Travel with carry-on only to get from the plane to your car faster.
  • If you're travelling as a group of 4 or more, compare a Sprinter van for the whole trip with buying separate plane tickets.
